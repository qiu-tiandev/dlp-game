This is a recycling game. There are 2 different types of trash [recyclable and general waste]. Drag the bins left and right to collect the trash! Use the keys "AD" and “←→” to control the blue bin and green bin respectively. 
For each correct match:
- Recyclable falls into the recycling bin.
- Non-recyclable falls into the general waste bins
- ADD 100 points!
For every wrong match:
- Recyclable falls into the general waste bin.
- Non-recyclable falls into the recycling bin.
- MINUS 1 heart
For every trash you do not collect and drop on the ground:
- You will lose 20 points
For every 1000 points you achieve:
- The speed of which the trash drop will increase!
- You can only make 4 mistakes as you only have 5 hearts!
